IronRoutePro Privacy Policy

Effective date: June 5, 2026

IronRoutePro is a motorcycle route planning and ride journal app. It is designed to be private by default: your data stays on your device.

Data We Collect

None. IronRoutePro does not collect, transmit, sell, or share any personal data. There are no analytics, no advertising identifiers, no tracking, and no crash reporting services in the app.

Data Stored on Your Device

The following is created by you and stored only locally on your device:

  • Profiles you create in the app (name, optional PIN)
  • Planned routes, waypoints, and map preferences
  • Ride journal entries and fuel fill-up logs
  • Documents you add to the Vault

This data never leaves your device unless you export or share it yourself. Deleting the app deletes this data.

Location

IronRoutePro requests location access (while using the app) to center the map, plan routes from your position, and estimate fuel range. Location data is processed entirely on your device and is never sent to us or to any third party.

Face ID / App Lock

If you enable the optional App Lock, authentication is handled by iOS (Face ID / Touch ID / passcode). Biometric data is managed by Apple's operating system and never leaves your device; the app only receives a success/failure result.

Map Tiles

To display maps, the app downloads map tiles from third-party map providers (such as OpenStreetMap-based tile servers). Like any internet request, these requests include your IP address. No account information, location history, or personal data is included in these requests.

Children

IronRoutePro is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this URL with a new effective date.
